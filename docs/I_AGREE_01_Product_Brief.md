# Product Brief — I AGREE: The Fine Print Decoder

**One-liner:** Paste any Terms of Service, privacy policy, or rental agreement and get a ten-second verdict — what data is taken, what rights you surrender, red flags ranked by severity — with every warning proven by the exact clause that caused it.

**Tagline:** *Every month you sign dozens of contracts you never read. We make them readable in ten seconds.*

**Context:** InnoVVate 2026 hackathon entry (Classes 6–9, theme "Invisible by Design"), due 5 Aug 2026, 9:00 pm IST. One version ships — there is no v2. Highest-scoring idea on the published judging criteria (35/40) with the lowest build risk.

## The problem

People click "I Agree" on 30–40 legal documents a month without reading a word — not from laziness, but because legalese is engineered to be unreadable. The consequences are real: resold data, silent auto-renewals, permissions nobody knew they granted.

## The product

- **Paste anything — or drop a link.** Terms, policies, agreements, consent text go into one box; a URL tab pulls the readable document from a link, falling back to paste when a site refuses.
- **Verdict card in ~10 seconds.** An overall Green/Amber/Red judgment, a one-line headline, and a five-bullet plain-language summary.
- **Three risk categories, ranked by severity:** data sharing, automatic renewal / difficult cancellation, and rights over user-created content. Deliberately narrow — depth over sprawl.
- **Proof behind every flag.** A plain-language explanation beside the exact verbatim clause, highlighted in the original document. Written so a Class 6 student can explain it back.
- **Honesty built in.** Every quote is programmatically verified to exist in the source before it is shown; anything unproven is demoted to "needs human review." A permanent line reads: *"This tool explains text; it does not replace legal advice."*
- **Instant on repeat.** Every analysed document is fingerprinted and cached — the same document returns instantly, and a public registry page lists recently decoded documents with their grades.
- **Shareable.** The headline and five bullets export as a clean image made to be sent to the family group chat.
- **Your history.** Sign in to keep a personal list of everything you've decoded; the app works fully signed out.
- **Instant demo documents.** Three real, credited public documents come pre-analysed and load instantly.

## Who it's for

Everyone who clicks Agree: students accepting app terms, parents signing bank and rent forms, grandparents facing consent screens.

## Differentiation

The AI is not the invention — the **verification layer** around it is: exact-clause citations, grouping of related clauses, sectioned handling of long documents, and fingerprint recognition of repeated documents. That is the answer to "isn't this just a prompt?"

## Not in this app

Legal advice, additional risk categories, a browser extension, multilingual support, a fairness index ranking apps — future ideas for the roadmap slide only.

## Success

All features live at one public URL, working on phone and laptop; every displayed quote matches its source exactly; a real, measured survey and comprehension result back the story; everything is credited.
